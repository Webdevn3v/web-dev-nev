Good to know

Common questions, compressed.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. Most major PPO plans are accepted, including Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Guardian, and Aetna. Benefits are verified before the appointment.

What if I haven’t been in years?

You will not be judged. The team will meet you where you are and create a realistic plan.

How long is a new patient visit?

Plan for about 60–75 minutes, including digital X-rays, an exam, possible cleaning, and time to discuss goals.

Do you see children?

Yes. Patients are welcomed from their first tooth, around age one or within six months of the first tooth appearing.

What payment options are available?

Major cards, cash, checks, CareCredit, and LendingClub patient financing are listed on the demo site.

How do I handle a dental emergency?

Call the office directly. Daily emergency slots are reserved for current patients; after-hours voicemail includes guidance and an emergency line for severe situations.
