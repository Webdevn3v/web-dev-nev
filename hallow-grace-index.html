Our Story

Three generations of gentle guidance

Since 1952, Hallow & Grace has served the families of Frederick County with quiet dignity and unwavering compassion. What began as a small family practice has grown into a trusted institution — but our values have never changed.

We believe that every life deserves to be honored fully, and every family deserves to feel held through one of life's most difficult transitions. We are here — not just to help you plan, but to walk alongside you.

What We Offer

Services offered with care

Every arrangement is handled personally. We offer a full range of services designed to honor your loved one in the way that feels most true to who they were.

Traditional Services

A full traditional service with visitation, funeral ceremony, and committal. We coordinate every detail — flowers, clergy, music, and transportation — so your family can simply be present.

Cremation Services

Dignified cremation options ranging from direct cremation to full memorial services. We guide you through every choice with patience and without pressure.

Memorial Celebrations

A personalized celebration of life that reflects who your loved one truly was. We help you create a gathering that feels meaningful, warm, and deeply personal.

Graveside Services

An intimate ceremony at the burial site. Simple, meaningful, and unhurried. We coordinate with cemeteries throughout the region on your behalf.

Veteran Honors

We are honored to serve those who served. Full military honors coordination, including flag presentation, bugle call, and burial in a veterans cemetery.

Transportation

Professional, dignified transportation for your loved one — locally and across state lines. Available 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Plan Ahead
