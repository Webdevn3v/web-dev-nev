What is this, exactly?

The Door is the experience behind the threshold.

The opening animation is the entrance. The Digital Door is what happens next: a branded, purpose-built starting point that recognizes the few reasons customers usually arrive and gives each one a clear route to the right action.

One Door can live in a lot of places.The usable QR file is handed over with the build, so the same Door can be reached from a business card, storefront, truck, sign, printed piece, or anywhere else customers need a direct way in.
Nothing replaces the full website. The Door handles the moment of intent; the in-depth site is still there when a customer wants the whole picture.

Good to knowOwnership, access + what happens next
+

You own what gets built.

The finished system, project files, QR asset, and handoff materials belong to the client. TDS project work is a one-time build payment — no required TDS subscription just to keep using what was purchased.

No passwords needed to start.

A new system can be built without access to personal accounts, inboxes, or existing logins. Existing access only becomes relevant when the project includes cleanup, migration, or organizing the business's digital junk drawer.

Third-party services stay in the client's name.

If a domain, business email, form service, or another outside service is needed, setup can be prepared as far as possible before the client completes any required ownership, billing, or verification step. Any future third-party renewal belongs to that service — not a TDS subscription.
