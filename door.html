Before we beginGood to know.
+
You own what gets built.+
The finished system, project files, QR asset, and handoff materials belong to the client. TDS project work is a one time payment. There is no required TDS subscription just to keep using what was purchased.
No passwords needed to start.+
A new system can be built without access to personal accounts, inboxes, or existing logins. Existing access only becomes relevant when the project includes cleanup, migration, or organizing the business's digital junk drawer.
Outside services stay in the client's name.+
If a domain, business email, form service, or another outside service is needed, setup can be prepared before the client completes any required ownership, billing, or verification step. Future outside service renewals belong to that service. They are not a TDS subscription.
Need keys for the crew?+
Team Key enhancements give designated staff members their own digital key or physical tap. Existing booking, ordering, payment, and communication tools can also be connected when they belong in the customer path.

Meet theDeveloper.
Nev GonzalezFounder & Lead Developer
Developer · Business Systems · Maryland

Hey, I’m Nev.

For nearly a decade, I worked across both sides of a small business in downtown Frederick, helping customers at the front while also managing the changing information, repeated questions, daily operations, and behind the scenes details that keep everything moving.

While studying Business Management and Forensic Accounting, I gained a stronger understanding of how those moving pieces fit together and where they can start to break down. A Salesforce internship added the technology side of business and opened the path into web development, which gave me a practical way to bring everything I had learned together.

That is where The Digital Side comes from: understanding how the business works first, then building the customer facing experience and digital systems around the way the brand actually operates.
