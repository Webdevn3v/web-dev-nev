05 / Good to knowOwnership, access + what happens next
+

The work is built to hand off cleanly.

Original project files, the usable QR asset and the pieces created for the business are organized around ownership — not keeping a client trapped behind a monthly website arrangement.

Passwords are not the product.

Most projects do not require handing over personal passwords. When an account connection is genuinely needed, access should be limited to the job and handled as deliberately as possible.

Payments stay with the payment processor.

Card details are not collected or stored by The Digital Side. Deposits and payments belong in the payment system, not inside a client form or project file.
