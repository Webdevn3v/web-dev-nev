What is this, exactly?

The useful part, first.

You hold the Digital Key — the one QR code your customers scan. It opens a Digital Door custom-coded around your business, then gives each customer a short path based on why they actually came. The destination is the useful part: the form, answer, call, booking, or next step they were looking for. No digging. No generic template pretending every business works the same way.
