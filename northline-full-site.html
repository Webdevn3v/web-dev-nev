Before work begins

Straight answers.

Do I need a repair or a replacement?

An inspection should answer that. Roof age matters, but water entry, substrate condition, flashing details, ventilation, and the extent of damage matter more.

Can Northline document storm damage?

Yes. The demo workflow includes condition photos and a written scope. Insurance coverage decisions remain with the carrier.

What should an estimate include?

Materials, tear-off, deck assumptions, ventilation, flashing, disposal, cleanup, scheduling, payment stages, and warranty information should all be clear.
