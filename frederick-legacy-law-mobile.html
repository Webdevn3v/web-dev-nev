Good to Know

Before the appointment.

Small details can make the first conversation much more useful.

Urgent matter?

If a hearing, filing, immediate deadline, or active estate issue is involved, call rather than waiting on email.

Coming to the office?

Confirm appointment details and parking instructions before arrival so the visit starts smoothly.

Do I need every document?

No. Bring what you have. Missing pieces can be identified after the situation is understood.

Not sure what service applies?

That is fine. Describe the situation in plain language and the matter can be routed from there.
