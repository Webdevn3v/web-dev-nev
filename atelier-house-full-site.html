Good to know.

The questions that come up before a first conversation.

How does a first consultation work?

We start with a conversation about how you use the space day to day, what's not working, and what you're drawn to — in person or virtually. From there we outline scope, timeline, and next steps before any design work begins.

Do you work with a single room, or only whole homes?

Both. Some projects are one room that needs pulling together; others are a full-home transformation. The scope is set around what you actually need, not a fixed package.

Do you source furniture and materials?

Yes. Sourcing, procurement, and trade-only access are part of the design process, so you're not left hunting down what was specified.

What if I already know what I want?

That's a faster path, not a problem — bring the reference images, the Pinterest board, or the one photo you keep going back to, and we build from there.

How long does a typical project take?

A single-room refresh can move in a matter of weeks; a full-home project is closer to several months once sourcing and installation are factored in. Timeline is confirmed during the initial scope conversation.
